# Frequently asked questions

**Do you offer price matching?**
No. Prices are fixed at the time of order and are not adjusted retroactively
if an item goes on sale afterwards.

**Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
Only if the order has not yet been marked as shipped. Once a tracking
number exists, the address can no longer be changed and the order must be
intercepted with the carrier or returned on arrival instead.

**Do you ship to PO boxes?**
Standard carrier shipments can be delivered to PO boxes. Freight-shipped
furniture cannot, since freight carriers require a physical delivery
address.

**What happens if my order arrives damaged?**
Damaged-on-arrival items follow the same rule as defective items under the
return policy: a return can be requested within 90 days of delivery,
original packaging is not required, and a refund is issued without first
requiring a replacement attempt.

**How is my loyalty tier calculated?**
Loyalty tier and risk score are computed from lifetime purchase value,
return count, and complaint count. They update automatically whenever an
order, return, or complaint is recorded; customers cannot request a
manual recalculation.
