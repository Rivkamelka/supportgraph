# Shipping policy

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country of
purchase, and 7 to 14 business days internationally. Express shipping
(1 to 2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee and
is only offered domestically.

Orders placed before 2pm local time ship the same business day. Orders
placed on weekends or public holidays ship the next business day.

A tracking link is emailed automatically as soon as a warehouse marks an
order as shipped. If a tracking link shows no movement for more than 5
business days, the order qualifies for a shipping investigation and, if the
package is confirmed lost, a free reshipment or full refund.

Furniture items (desks, chairs) ship via a separate freight carrier and can
take up to 10 business days domestically; this is disclosed on the product
page and is not considered a delay unless that window is exceeded.
