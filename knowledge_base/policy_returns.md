# Return policy

Customers may return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided it is in its original packaging. Items marked as defective on
arrival (dead pixels, broken cables, non-functional units) can be returned
at any time within the first 90 days, and do not require the original
packaging.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business
days of the warehouse receiving the returned item. Store credit is offered
instead of a refund only if the customer explicitly requests it.

Digital goods and gift cards are not eligible for return.

A return does not require a replacement to be attempted first: if a
customer prefers a refund over a replacement for a defective item, the
refund is approved directly.
